\section{Motivation}

Counterfactual explanation methods answer the question practitioners ask in
high-stakes sequential settings: \emph{what would have had to be different
in this trajectory for the model to predict otherwise?} The temporal CF
literature scores its answers with validity, proximity, sparsity, and
OOD-plausibility --- every one of which measures the CF against
\textbf{the model}, none against \textbf{the process that generated the
data}.

That gap is unmeasurable without a benchmark that knows its own mechanism.
This work builds one and uses it to ask: \emph{when a CF method claims a
change would produce an outcome, does the world agree?}

On every wired method the answer is \textbf{no} or \textbf{only vacuously
yes} --- separating those two is the instrument's job. Under the
whole-proposal reading, methods buy claimed validity by declaring most of
the trajectory as interventions (do-complexity $D$ near $T$). A
single-$\mathrm{do}()$ recourse control is the only method proposing an
intervention in the usual sense of the word.

\section{Scope}

\begin{quote}
A benchmark and audit protocol for counterfactual explanation methods on
time series, evaluated on whether their claimed causal efficacy is realised
by the data-generating process.
\end{quote}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{CF explainers only.} Attribution (TimeSHAP, Dynamask) and
        concept-based methods (TCAV-T, CBM-T, LEAP, iVAE, $\beta$-VAE) are
        out of scope. The former concept axis (ICC, MIG, DCI, MCC) was
        deleted 2026-08-03; axis letters were reassigned (\S\ref{sec:protocol}).
        Concept stability is dropped with it.
  \item \textbf{Causal-graph access is an assumption, not a discovery
        problem.} On the synthetic tiers the graph is the generator's
        ground truth. Discovery is load-bearing only in deferred Tier~3,
        where one graph is propagated to both methods and metrics.
  \item \textbf{Robustness is its own axis}, not a grab-bag shared with
        attribution diagnostics.
\end{itemize}

Proposing a method that scores well against this protocol is the natural
follow-up. Nothing here depends on it.

\section{Gap in the Literature}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Temporal XAI benchmarks} (CFTS, XTSC-Bench, AMEE, CARLA):
        validity, proximity, sparsity, OOD --- no causal faithfulness, no
        way to ask whether a proposed intervention \emph{works}.
  \item \textbf{Temporal causal discovery benchmarks} (CausalDynamics,
        CausalTime, CITRIS): graph recovery, no explanation method in the
        loop.
  \item \textbf{Necessity/sufficiency for explanation} (LEWIS; Watson
        et~al.\ 2021): the right question, asked in the static setting,
        necessarily answered with Tian--Pearl \emph{bounds} because both
        potential outcomes are never jointly observed.
  \item \textbf{SCM-regularised temporal CF} (Bahri et~al., IEEE BigData
        2025): the SOTA in causally-motivated temporal CF. It uses the SCM
        to \textbf{penalise} implausible edits --- a residual
        $\lVert T'_t - f(\mathrm{Pa}(T'_t))\rVert$ added to a
        Wachter-style objective over a free perturbation $T' = T + \Delta$.
        No $\mathrm{do}()$, no intervention time, no abduction.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Related-work framing:} the field uses SCMs to
\textbf{penalise} counterfactuals; nobody uses them to \textbf{derive} the
counterfactual a proposal is scored against.

\noindent\textbf{The delta:} in the temporal additive-noise setting,
abduction is exact subtraction, so both potential outcomes are computable
and necessity/sufficiency is \textbf{exact rather than bounded} --- plus a
model-vs-world decomposition separating ``the CF is not what the world
would produce'' from ``the classifier does not track the true label
rule''.

A residual penalty is a \emph{local one-step} check: it never propagates
the intervention and never abducts the factual noise. A CF that is locally
mechanism-consistent at every timestep while its intervention has
\textbf{no effect on the outcome} scores perfectly. That is the
\texttt{NoiselessSCMRecourse} pathology (CF-faith $1.00$, validity $1.00$,
intervention effect $\sim 2.6{\times}10^{-4}$): the strongest published
causal-CF criterion is structurally blind to the failure mode
\S\ref{sec:contrib} measures.

The surviving novelty is structural ground truth \textbf{as an evaluation
oracle for independently produced, post-hoc CF explainers} --- not the
artifact of shipping a ground-truth DAG with classification labels, which
other work already provides.

\section{Contributions}
\label{sec:contrib}

Ordered by strength. Each is defensible if the others are challenged, and
\textbf{none requires any evaluated method to succeed.}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{A model-vs-world audit instrument for temporal CF
        explanations.} For a proposed CF, the same intervention is
        realised by the true mechanism and the two outcomes compared,
        decomposing the disagreement additively into a trajectory term and
        an outcome term. Necessity and sufficiency are computed
        \textbf{exactly}, not bounded. Because the audit's answer depends
        on how the proposal is read, the benchmark reports
        \textbf{do-complexity $D$} --- the number of timesteps a proposal
        must declare as actions before the mechanism can reproduce it ---
        beside $\Deltatraj$.

        \emph{Headline (restated 2026-08-03).} The earlier claim ``no
        wired method makes a causal intervention,
        $\Deltatraj \approx 1.0$'' was an artifact of the single-slice
        reading and is \textbf{retracted}. What replaces it: methods buy
        claimed validity by intervening almost everywhere. Pooled over 3
        seeds on \texttt{full} ($T = 100$), $D$ reads
        \texttt{PearlSCMRecourse} $\mathbf{1.0}$,
        \texttt{CftsCels} $6.6$,
        \texttt{NoiselessSCMRecourse} $69.7$,
        \texttt{CftsCOMTE} $83.3$,
        \texttt{CftsWachter} $96.4$,
        \texttt{CftsConfeti} $\mathbf{100.0}$.
        \texttt{CftsWachter} fails both readings ($D = 96.4$ \textbf{and}
        $\Deltatotal = 0.39$). The single-$\mathrm{do}()$ control is the
        only method at $D = 1$.

  \item \textbf{Intervention-to-outcome distance as a required reporting
        condition.} SCM stability requires spectral radius $< 1$, so an
        intervention's effect on the label attenuates geometrically in the
        distance from $t_0$ to the \textbf{label site} $t_{\mathrm{label}}$,
        not automatically to $T$. Recourse feasibility is a property of
        the benchmark configuration, not of the method; beyond a
        computable horizon no causally valid recourse exists, and any
        validity reported there exploits a non-causal artifact. The
        contribution is the diagnostic and the reporting rule, not an
        impossibility claim.

  \item \textbf{A benchmark suite with exact structural counterfactuals.}
        \textbf{LinearSCM-T} (VAR($L$)) and \textbf{NlinearSCM-T}
        (per-node additive-noise MLP transitions), each shipping true
        graph, true mechanism, and oracle
        abduction--action--prediction counterfactuals.
        \textbf{SpringSCM-T} is the non-dissipative family used to attack
        the horizon claim. The label functional is a configurable axis,
        which is what makes the distance reporting rule testable.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{CF-faith is the admission gate, not a fourth
contribution.} Two mutually exclusive semantics
(\texttt{noiseless\_rollout}, \texttt{pearl\_delta}) --- a single CF
cannot be hard-faithful under both --- but it is a pass/fail check with no
dynamic range across real methods. Listing it as a contribution invites
evaluation as a ranking metric. Rankings by traditional CF metrics vs
CF-faith are a \textbf{demoted descriptive observation} (H6), not the key
finding: CF-faith has no dynamic range across real methods, so the
correlation is structurally underpowered.

\section{Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\textbf{Three axes, and every reported metric belongs to exactly one}
(map: \texttt{causaltemp\_xai/metrics/taxonomy.py}):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Axis} & \textbf{Scores} & \textbf{Unit} \\
  \midrule
  A --- structure & the benchmark & per \textbf{dataset} \\
  B --- robustness & methods & per method \\
  C --- counterfactual quality & methods & per method \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Axis~A characterises the data-generating process; B and C characterise
explainers. Presenting all three as parallel per-method scores is a
category error. The former Axis~A (concept/representation quality) was
deleted with the concept-based methods it scored. There is no Axis~D;
robustness was relettered into B.

\subsection{Axis C --- Counterfactual Quality \emph{(per method)}}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Validity} --- fraction of CFs that flip the classifier.
        Must be reported alongside the intervention-to-outcome distance;
        it is not interpretable without it.
  \item \textbf{Proximity} --- $\lVert \hat{x}^{n} - x^{n} \rVert_{p}$
        ($L_1 / L_2$).
  \item \textbf{Sparsity} --- fraction of the $T \times k$ features left
        \textbf{unchanged} ($\lvert\Delta\rvert \le
        \texttt{INTERVENTION\_TOL}$); \textbf{higher $=$ sparser edit},
        $1.0$ $=$ CF identical to the original. Complement:
        \texttt{frac\_altered}. Structured variants
        \texttt{sparsity\_channels} / \texttt{sparsity\_timepoints}
        report channels (resp.\ timesteps) left entirely untouched.
  \item \textbf{OOD plausibility} --- Isolation Forest (LOF specified;
        Isolation Forest is the wired estimator).
  \item \textbf{CF-faith} \emph{(admission gate)} ---
        no-retroactive-change $\wedge$ graph-propagation, under
        \texttt{noiseless\_rollout} \emph{or} \texttt{pearl\_delta}.
        Hard (indicator) and soft ($\exp(-\mathrm{residual}/\mathrm{scale})$)
        variants. Returns \textbf{NaN}, not $1.0$, when
        $\texttt{intervention\_t} \ge T-1$; callers report
        \texttt{frac\_degenerate} / \texttt{n\_cf\_faith\_scorable}
        alongside every value.
  \item \textbf{TRSI} --- Temporal Relevance Smoothness Index;
        mechanism-free descriptor of edit coherence. \textbf{Not} claimed
        as a contribution. Not T-stab (deleted; never implemented).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Necessity / Sufficiency Audit --- PNS \emph{(per method)}}

For a factual $x$, a method's CF $x_{\mathrm{cf}}$, and
$t_0 = \mathrm{derive\_intervention\_t}(x, x_{\mathrm{cf}})$
(the same intervention CF-faith scores):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Outcome fn} & \textbf{Trajectory} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
  \midrule
  $A$ & classifier & method's $x_{\mathrm{cf}}$ & what the explanation \textbf{claims} \\
  $B$ & classifier & oracle CF of the same intervention & the claim, on the world's trajectory \\
  $C$ & SCM label rule & oracle CF of the same intervention & what the world \textbf{does} \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{align}
  \Deltatotal      &= A - C, \\
  \Deltatraj       &= A - B
    \quad\text{(proposed CF is not what the world would produce),} \\
  \Deltaout        &= B - C
    \quad\text{(classifier does not track the true label rule),} \\
  \Deltatotal      &= \Deltatraj + \Deltaout.
\end{align}

Signed; both directions informative: $> 0$ the explanation claims an
efficacy the world denies; $< 0$ the model is blind to a real dependence.
The oracle uses \textbf{Pearl semantics} (re-injecting abducted factual
noise), because the question is what would have happened \emph{to this
instance}.

\textbf{Do-complexity $D$ is reported on every row.} A $\Delta$ of either
kind must never be cited without it. $D = 0$ is the vacuous case;
\texttt{frac\_vacuous} is the $D = 0$ row of the same distribution.

Both directions are estimated --- \textbf{PS} on flip-candidates,
\textbf{PN} on the complement --- and combined as
$\mathrm{PNS} = P(x,y)\,\mathrm{PN} + P(x',y')\,\mathrm{PS}$.
PN, PS, and the mixing weights are \textbf{always reported separately}:
on flip-candidates alone the model side collapses into validity, so a
combined-only report would misname the quantity.

\subsection{Axis B --- Robustness \emph{(per method)}}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Shift-VR} --- validity retention on held-out environments
        $\Etest$ (wired: same SCM, uniform noise; frozen classifier).
  \item \textbf{Input sensitivity} --- Lipschitz constant w.r.t.\ the
        input, where exposed. Specified; not a live pipeline column for
        the CF-only method set.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Axis A --- Structure \emph{(per dataset)}}

SHD, lag accuracy, lagged-edge F1, residual dependence, graph-error
decomposition, AUC-ROC against ground truth --- computed once per dataset
in Phase~01. It characterises the \emph{benchmark}, not any method.

The diagnostic is live post-retune (a random graph costs $0.748$; a
well-recovered DYNOTEARS graph still costs $0.163$--$0.687$). It must not
be printed as a per-method score alongside B and C.

\section{Why CF-faith Is a Gate and $\Deltatotal$ Ranks}

CF-faith (hard) is an \textbf{indicator}. Every method that is not a
positive control fails it identically. On \texttt{full},
\texttt{NoiselessSCMRecourse} scores $1.00$ (tautological: it emits the
noiseless rollout it is scored against), \texttt{PearlSCMRecourse} is
NaN under the noiseless semantics, and every other method is exactly
$0.00$. Ranking that column measures ``is this the oracle'', not method
quality.

PNS ranks at smoke scale ($T = 30$) and not at paper scale ($T = 100$):
at $t_0 \in \{25, 50\}$ before a terminal label at $T = 100$,
$\mathrm{PN}_{\mathrm{world}} = 0$ for all methods --- zero causal
effect, not a small one. \textbf{What ranks at paper scale is
$\Deltatotal$}, per direction, read with $D$.

Therefore: CF-faith admits or rejects; $\Deltatotal$ and do-complexity
rank; PNS is reportable where the horizon leaves it non-degenerate
(smoke) and reported as a null with both terms shown where it does not
(\texttt{full}); and $t_{\mathrm{label}} - t_0$ conditions the
interpretation of all of them.

\section{Benchmarks}

Tiers are \textbf{external validity} for the findings, not validation of
the metrics. A metric can only be shown faithful where a ground-truth
mechanism exists --- Tier~1 and nowhere else. Tiers~2 and~3 cannot tell
whether CF-faith, PNS, or $D$ measure what they claim; they test whether
the Tier~1 phenomenon travels. Increasing realism buys generality, not
construct validity.

\subsection{Tier 1 --- Synthetic, ground-truth graph}

The only tier where the causal claim, and every metric-validity argument,
is established.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{LinearSCM-T} --- VAR($L$). Design space:
        $k \in \{5, 10, 20\}$,
        $L \in \{1, 3\}$,
        sparsity $s/k^{2} \in \{0.1, 0.2, 0.3\}$,
        non-Gaussian noise (Laplace, uniform),
        $T \in \{50, 100, 200\}$, $N = 10{,}000$.
        Ships with true graph, true mechanism, and oracle CF trajectories.
        Observation is identity: nonlinear mixing $x = g(z)$ is
        deliberately deferred.

  \item \textbf{NlinearSCM-T} --- same structure, per-node MLP
        transitions
        $x_t^i = \mathrm{decay}_i \cdot x_{t-1}^i
        + \mathrm{gain}\cdot\tanh(\mathrm{MLP}_i(\text{masked lags}))
        + \varepsilon_t^i$,
        spectral-norm-capped. \textbf{Additive noise is load-bearing}:
        Pearl abduction remains exact subtraction, so CF-faith, the
        oracle structural CF, and exact PNS carry over unchanged.
        Nonlinear \emph{transitions}, not nonlinear mixing.

  \item \textbf{SpringSCM-T} --- 2nd-order Newtonian (Kipf et~al.\ NRI,
        as used by Bahri et~al.). Conservative dynamics (measured
        $\rho = +0.024$ vs \texttt{full\_nl}'s $-0.689$) with
        terminal-time labels, putting the horizon claim in the regime
        where effects persist. Additive-noise, so the protocol carries
        over. KuramotoSCM-T was removed 2026-08-11.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Registered paper presets} currently lock $L = 1$
(\texttt{smoke}: $k{=}5$, $T{=}30$, $N{=}500$;
\texttt{full} / \texttt{full\_nl}: $k{=}10$, $T{=}100$, $N{=}10{,}000$;
\texttt{smoke\_spring}: $k{=}10$, $T{=}30$). Uniform noise is the
Shift-VR environment, not a separate paper cell. Changing $L$ requires
revisiting VAR stability and is not a casual preset edit.

\subsection{Tier 2 --- Real signal, horizon external validity}

UCR/UEA multivariate subset (BasicMotions, RacketSports, Epilepsy) used
for \textbf{one} purpose: testing whether the horizon result holds
outside synthetic SCMs. Requires a classifier, a label site, and a
measurable intervention-to-outcome distance --- \textbf{no causal
graph}. Discovered-graph CF-faith on real data is a narrow disclosed
exception (variance quantified by a DYNOTEARS ensemble; bias from the
linear-SEM assumption is not resolved).

\subsection{Tier 3 --- SepsisSim \emph{(deferred)}}

MIMIC-IV sepsis cohort with a clinician-validated DAG is \textbf{not} a
current contribution. A dataset- and graph-source-agnostic orchestrator
exists as machinery; it is not a commitment to SepsisSim. If later
green-lit, this is the only tier where discovery is load-bearing, and
its graph-error is then the ceiling on everything scored against it.

\section{Hypotheses}

Short form. Renumbered 2026-08-06 to six entries, ordered by strength.
Closed pre-renumbering IDs (H3a, H8, H8b, H8c, H9) are historical
record only.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=*, style=nextline]
  \item[H1.] Standard temporal CF methods achieve high validity ($> 0.9$)
    with CF-faith $\approx 0$.
    \emph{Split verdict:} the CF-faith clause is universal (all four
    wired standard explainers score $0.000$ on \texttt{full}). Validity
    $> 0.9$ is not --- only COMTE and Wachter clear it; CELS and CONFETI
    do not. None buy causal faithfulness with whatever validity they
    achieve.

  \item[H2.] Given a well-recovered graph, the oracle structural CF
    retains near-full CF-faith, so explainer failures are
    \textbf{propagation} failures, not graph-estimation failures.
    \textbf{Refuted 2026-08-12.} A random graph costs $0.748$;
    DYNOTEARS's own graph costs $0.547$ at AUC $0.965$ / SHD~$4$. Graph
    estimation is a first-order error source. ``Graph quality is not the
    problem'' must not appear in the manuscript.

  \item[H2$'$.] \emph{(Post-hoc, not pre-registered.)} Explainer choice
    spans a wider range of mechanism-infidelity than graph quality does,
    in both contraction regimes. A claim about dynamic range only; it
    does \textbf{not} assert graph error is negligible.

  \item[H3.] \emph{(Lead empirical claim.)} For every wired CF method,
    $\Deltatotal > 0$, attributable almost entirely to $\Deltatraj$:
    the proposed CF is not what the world would produce, and when the
    world realises the method's own intervention the classifier does not
    flip.

  \item[H4.] Recourse validity decays with the intervention-to-outcome
    distance at a rate governed by the SCM's spectral radius; beyond a
    computable horizon no valid causal recourse exists, and any validity
    there is a non-causal artifact of the CF semantics.
    Evidence~(i): non-dissipative families show no such decay (validity
    \emph{increases} with horizon). Evidence~(ii): validity and PN decay
    with distance to the \textbf{label site}, not with trajectory
    length --- confirmed at smoke scale, weakly supported at paper
    scale.

  \item[H5.] \emph{(Retrospective synthesis, not itself pre-registered.)}
    The validity--CF-faith divergence is invariant to noise family
    (Gaussian vs Laplace), mechanism monotonicity ($\tanh$ vs $\sin$),
    and regime stationarity --- a property of the additive-noise causal
    structure, not of any one generalisation axis.

  \item[H6.] Rankings by traditional CF metrics do not track rankings by
    CF-faith. \textbf{Demoted} to a descriptive observation:
    underpowered and structurally hard to power, since CF-faith has no
    dynamic range across real methods. Must never be labelled the key
    finding.
\end{description}

\noindent\textbf{Not a hypothesis --- construction check.} Causal
recourse attaining CF-faith $> 0.7$ is entailed by definition:
\texttt{NoiselessSCMRecourse} emits the noiseless rollout it is scored
against, and \texttt{PearlSCMRecourse} has $\Deltatraj = 0$ because the
oracle is a Pearl rollout of the same intervention. The non-tautological
quantity for \texttt{PearlSCMRecourse} is $C$ --- whether the world
delivers the outcome change --- and only $C$ may be cited.

\section{Scope Boundaries}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=*, style=nextline]
  \item[O1. Concept / representation quality.] Former Axis~A (ICC, MIG,
    DCI, MCC) removed with its code. Reinstating means reinstating an
    axis, not restoring a module.
  \item[O2. Attribution-method evaluation.] No axis scores them.
  \item[O3. Latent confounding.] Causal sufficiency is assumed;
    unmeasured confounders are future work.
  \item[O4. Nonlinear mixing and non-additive noise.]
    NlinearSCM-T covers nonlinear \emph{transitions} with additive
    noise, which is what keeps abduction exact. Relaxing additivity
    costs the exactness \S3 identifies as this work's delta.
  \item[O5. Horizon limit.] With spectral radius $< 1$, configurations
    exist in which \emph{no} method can succeed. Every validity and PNS
    number is reported with $T - t_0$ \textbf{and}
    $t_{\mathrm{label}} - t_0$.
  \item[O6. Intervention reading.] Neither the single-slice nor the
    whole-schedule reading is privileged. Only the pair $(D, \Delta)$
    is reportable.
  \item[O7. Label functional.] $C$, $\Deltaout$, PN, and PS are
    properties of a design choice, not a fact about the world. Default
    is a terminal single-channel threshold.
  \item[O8. CF-faith has no dynamic range across real methods.] The
    gate rejects every non-control entrant; that is a finding, not a
    leaderboard.
  \item[O9. $\mathtt{graph\_error}$ is not scale-free.] It is a
    difference of $\exp(-\mathrm{residual})$ soft scores. Cross-config
    comparison requires the $\mathtt{graph\_error\_sigma}$ companion.
  \item[O10. Tier 3 only.] Causal-discovery accuracy is the ceiling on
    everything scored against a discovered graph. SepsisSim / MIMIC-IV
    / continuous-time hybrid systems remain deferred; there is no
    Neural-ODE clinical benchmark in the current suite.
  \item[O11. Computational tractability.] Joint-training efficiency of
    causal methods is not benchmarked.
\end{description}
