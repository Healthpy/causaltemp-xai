## 4. Metrics: hard vs soft faithfulness

Two faithfulness variants implemented (design decision: ship both): **rollout** and **Pearl's**, each with **hard** and **soft** scoring → 4 metrics. Score returns binary + continuous.

- **Hard:** understood and trusted.
- **Soft:** takes negative residuals → `exp(·)` → values in [0,1]. **Concern:** it's just a difference, ignoring temporal ordering / how effects propagate over time; unclear how to interpret as "more causal." Scores come out relatively high but hard to justify.
  - **Open question:** reformulate soft into something causally meaningful (use collaborator's causality expertise). Everything works without soft for now — parked as a research question.
