### Verdict

**The central claim does not survive unmodified, but a narrowed version survives comfortably and is, if anything, strengthened by how close the nearest prior work (#9, #10, #11 above) now is.** Recommend the manuscript state the contribution as:

> *No existing public benchmark jointly provides (i) ground-truth causal variables, (ii) a ground-truth lagged causal graph, (iii) classification labels, **and** (iv) analytically exact structural counterfactuals **used as an evaluation oracle for independently-produced, post-hoc CF-explanation methods**, in a temporal classification setting.*

This is exactly the narrowing direction the PI's plan already anticipated (R6, §4 M6 text: "first with analytical structural-CF ground truth for classification specifically") — this pass confirms that direction is necessary (CAUKER and the Causal-DAG-Prior paper already jointly cover (i)+(ii)+classification-labels; DoFlow already covers (iii) for forecasting) and sufficient (nothing found provides the explainer-evaluation harness itself). **Risk if not narrowed:** a reviewer who knows CAUKER/the Causal-DAG-Prior paper (both very recent, high-visibility ICLR/near-ICLR work) will immediately ask "isn't this already done?" — the honest answer is "the data-generation half, yes; the CF-explainer-evaluation half, no," and the manuscript needs to say that explicitly rather than let the reviewer discover it. **Action items before manuscript framing (M7):** (a) add CausalDynamics, TimeGraph, XTSC-Bench, and AMEE to the related-work table even though none of them threaten the claim, purely for completeness and reviewer credibility; (b) give CAUKER and the Causal-DAG-Prior paper their own explicit "closest prior work" paragraph with the delta stated in one sentence each; (c) do a full (not abstract-only) read of CausalProfiler before final submission — it is the one item here I could not fully rule out with the tools available today.

Confidence in this overall verdict: **Medium-High** — based on a same-day, broad-but-not-exhaustive web search (no institutional Scopus/Web of Science access used) against a live, fast-moving 2025–2026 literature; recommend the lab re-run this check once more, close to the actual M7 manuscript-freeze date, since several of the closest works (CAUKER, the Causal-DAG-Prior paper, CausalProfiler) are themselves only weeks to months old as of today (2026-07-08) and the field is visibly active in exactly this niche.
